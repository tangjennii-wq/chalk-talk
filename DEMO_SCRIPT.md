# Chalk Talk · 60-second demo script

A tight, screen-recorded demo for Twitter / LinkedIn / grand rounds. The whole thing runs ~60 seconds. Designed to hook a busy attending in the first 3 seconds and end with a clear "try it now" URL.

---

## Pre-record checklist

- **Browser**: Chrome or Safari, full-screen, no extensions visible. Hide bookmarks bar.
- **Window**: Resize to ~1280×800 for a clean 16:9 export. Zoom to 110-120% for legibility.
- **Setup**: Have your live URL (`https://tangjennii-wq.github.io/chalk-talk/`) loaded with the empty state visible.
- **Demo topic**: Use **"Hyponatremia: SIADH pathophysiology & Furst ratio"** — recognizable to every internist, dramatic Visual Memory Card output, fast generation.
- **Audio**: Record voiceover separately (QuickTime Player → New Audio Recording) for cleaner audio. Built-in MacBook mic is fine — speak ~6 inches away in a quiet room.
- **Recording tool**: QuickTime Player → File → New Screen Recording → record full screen or selected window. Save as `.mov`, then trim/export to `.mp4` or `.gif` via QuickTime or any video editor.

## Filming tips

- **Use a slow, deliberate cursor.** Hand-recording in real-time looks frantic. Use [Cursor Pro](https://cursor.so) or just slow your hand down 30%.
- **Trim the loading time.** Even a 30-second generation is too long for a 60-second video. Cut to a quick "loading…" beat (1-2s), then jump to the rendered talk.
- **Zoom in on the citation tooltip.** That moment is the most credibility-establishing — viewers need to *see* that the tooltip says "KDIGO 2024" not "made up by AI."
- **End on the URL frozen on screen for ~3 seconds.** People take a beat to read URLs.
- **Caption everything.** ~80% of social videos play muted. Use auto-captions in CapCut or Descript, then proof-read.

## After you record

1. Upload the `.mp4` to Twitter / LinkedIn natively (don't link to YouTube — natives autoplay)
2. Pin the tweet on your profile
3. Tag a few medical educators (e.g. @AvitalONeil, @DxRxEdu, @Cardionerds, @TLDRpharmacy)
4. Cross-post to /r/Residency on Reddit (mod-approval gated)
5. Submit to [MedEdPORTAL](https://www.mededportal.org/) for peer-reviewed publication

The video alone won't carry the post — pair it with the tweet copy above and a clear ask ("Would love your feedback").
