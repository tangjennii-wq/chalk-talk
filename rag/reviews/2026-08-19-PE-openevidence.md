# Acute PE — OpenEvidence review of the generated card (2026-08-19, via Jenni)

Five points. Read them as CORPUS work, not talk work: four of the five are facts the
guideline entry should carry, so fixing the entry fixes every future PE talk instead
of this one card. Same shape as the DKA finding — the corpus is silent or stale, and
the model fills the gap from memory.

## 1. A-E categories: the reperfusion trigger is wrong
The card maps "high-risk (shock/arrest) -> lysis, intermediate-high -> monitor".
The 2026 guideline anchors advanced therapy to **Category E1 (reasonable)** and
**D1-2 (may be considered)** - i.e. incipient cardiopulmonary failure / normotensive
shock, NOT only overt shock or arrest. This is the new decision axis and the card's
Section 3 is built on the old one.

## 2. Catheter-directed therapy is understated
Card says CDT is "evolving (ULTIMA, PE-TRACT)". Correct that ULTIMA is the only
completed RCT (faster RV/LV reduction at 24 h, no mortality difference, endpoint of
uncertain clinical relevance). Missing: observational meta-analyses suggest lower
mortality and less bleeding vs systemic lysis; the guideline positions CDT as an
option for D1-2/E1 and **explicitly does not recommend it for stable C2-3** (no
deterioration benefit, more bleeding than anticoagulation alone).

## 3. Recurrence denominator is wrong
Card: "10% at 1 year, 30% at 5 years". Sources: 10% at 1 year and **36% at 10 years**
for unprovoked PE (NEJM review); 2026 guideline cites ~30-40% at 10 years. The
5-year figure should be re-based to the 10-year denominator or dropped.

## 4. Extended-phase dosing needs its sequencing stated
Reduced-dose apixaban 2.5 mg BID / rivaroxaban 10 mg daily is right FOR THE EXTENDED
PHASE - but only after completing the initial 3-6 month full-dose treatment phase.
Stated without that, it reads as a starting dose. Dangerous omission, not a nuance.

## 5. Fluid caution is too absolute
"Avoid large boluses, use norepinephrine, consider inhaled pulmonary vasodilators" is
directionally right for RV failure, but a cautious challenge (<=500 mL) may be
reasonable in a non-congested RV. Soften from a blanket prohibition.

## Teaching aid
The guideline's radial Central Illustration operationalises A-E into escalating tiers
(anticoagulation -> PERT -> advanced therapies). Worth mirroring in the card's structure.
